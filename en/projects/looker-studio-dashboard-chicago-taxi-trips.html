Project 05 · BI dashboard

Looker Studio Dashboard · Chicago Taxi Trips

✅ publisheddata: 2026

Interactive dashboard covering taxi trips reported in Chicago between January and March 2026. It brings together indicators of trips, revenue, duration and distance, and lets users explore their distribution by time of day, company and city area. Navigation combines an operational overview, variable analysis, origin–destination journeys and geospatial maps.

CONTEXT AND OBJECTIVE

The project uses records from Chicago Taxi Trips, the public source of taxi trips reported to the city. Each record provides information on dates, origin and destination areas, duration, distance, fare, tips, payment method and company, depending on data availability. The figures shown correspond to the period and filters selected in the report.

The objective is to turn this operational information into a tool for visual exploration. The dashboard organises the analysis into four views: overview, variable analysis, journeys and geospatial analysis, with controls for comparing results by date, day of the week, time of day, company and payment method.

Reading the indicators together helps explain what lies behind each variation: higher revenue may be associated with the number of trips, distances travelled or amounts charged. Combining charts and filters makes it easier to compare these patterns across times and areas of the city.

DATA AND DASHBOARD DEVELOPMENT

PREPARATION AND NAVIGATION

The data was prepared in Python using data type checks and outlier filters defined by business criteria. Trips with extreme durations, distances, speeds or amounts that could distort the distributions and charts were excluded. Temporal and geographical variables were also organised.

The main limits applied were:

  • Duration: between 90 and 5,400 seconds.
  • Distance travelled: between 0.30 and 50 miles.
  • Average speed: between 1 and 70 miles per hour.
  • Base fare: between 3.25 and 150 dollars.
  • Fare per mile: between 2 and 50 dollars; fare per minute: between 0.30 and 5 dollars.
  • Total trip amount: up to 190 dollars.

Navigation connects the four views, allowing users to move from the general overview to exploring variables, journeys and maps. Each page combines charts, tables and controls that support analysis of reported trips from different perspectives.

OVERVIEW AND KEY INDICATORS

The opening page summarises activity for the selected period through cards showing trips, revenue, fares, costs, distance and duration. Charts on the same screen allow comparisons of daily activity, companies and service usage patterns.

The date selector changes the period analysed, while the MENU button provides access to the other dashboard pages. Filters for month, day, time of day, company and payment method update the visualisations according to the selection.

Time-based visualisations show daily changes in trips and revenue, the distribution of trip durations and company shares. Afternoon accounts for 53% of trips and morning for 30%. Mobile payments represent 42.5% of trips in the displayed data selection, followed by credit cards at 28.6%.

VARIABLE ANALYSIS

The second view examines how speed, total cost and fares per minute and per mile change throughout the day. Each indicator shows the median alongside the 25th and 75th percentiles, revealing the range containing the middle half of trips.

Median speed is higher in the early hours and falls during the busiest daytime periods. Total cost and fare per minute also vary by time, while fare per mile provides another perspective on journeys. Comparing these measures helps distinguish changes in duration, distance and amounts, even between time slots with similar trip counts.

ORIGIN–DESTINATION JOURNEYS

The third view connects departure neighbourhoods and areas with arrival areas using tables and Sankey diagrams. It includes origin and destination rankings, details of origin–destination pairs and flows associated with companies. It also compares trip counts, total fares and cumulative minutes for each route.

Near North Side accounts for 26.9% of origins and 28.5% of destinations in the displayed data selection. Trips starting and ending within Near North Side total around 47.2 thousand, equivalent to 9.27% of the sample shown. These indicators reveal both movement within one area and connections between Chicago areas.

GEOSPATIAL ANALYSIS

The fourth view brings together maps of pickup and drop-off concentrations, tips by destination and journeys between origin and arrival areas. The route visualisation allows users to select areas and study the most frequent connections within the city.

Other maps compare total cost per mile, cost per minute, median speed, duration, fare and distance by origin area. Geographical information is interpreted at the level of the areas recorded by the source, helping identify spatial differences in reported trips.

DELIVERABLES AND LEARNING

The result is a public dashboard with four connected analytical views. The overview summarises volume and revenue; variable analysis shows differences by time of day; tables and Sankey diagrams describe relationships between areas and companies; and maps allow exploration of the geographical distribution.

The concentration of afternoon trips, the prominence of Near North Side among origins and destinations, and hourly variations in speed and costs show how the same service can be analysed from complementary perspectives. Combining indicators, distributions and maps helps interpret both activity levels and journey characteristics.

The analysis relates trip counts to speed and fares by time of day. Medians and percentiles show the variations; origin–destination flows and maps locate these differences within Chicago. This approach helps distinguish operational profiles across areas.
