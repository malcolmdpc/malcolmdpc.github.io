Project 05 · BI dashboard

Looker Studio Dashboard · Chicago Taxi Trips

✅ publisheddata: 2026

Interactive Looker Studio dashboard for exploring Chicago taxi trips, operational indicators, hourly patterns and pickup-dropoff routes.

CONTEXT AND OBJECTIVE

Design an interactive view to explore the behavior of reported Chicago taxi trips through operational indicators and route patterns.

DATA AND DIAGNOSIS

  • A processed dataset prepared for analytical consumption is used.
  • Summary metrics, time segmentations and territorial cuts are built.
  • Interactive visuals are designed to support exploration, comparison and executive reading.

DELIVERY AND LEARNING

The delivery turns the analysis into a navigable dashboard designed to review operational signals without going through notebook or code.
