Project 11 · Football Analytics

2022 World Cup Statistics: Percentile Radar

✅ publisheddata: 2022

Analysis of 2022 FIFA World Cup statistics using public StatsBomb data, focused on comparing individual performances through percentile radar charts.

CONTEXT AND OBJECTIVE

Understand how individual performances from the Qatar 2022 FIFA World Cup can be summarized from match events and compared across players.

DATA AND DIAGNOSIS

  • Public StatsBomb data is used as the event source for the Qatar 2022 World Cup.
  • Variables are prepared and aggregated by player, team and match context.
  • Radar charts are built to compare player performance profiles and valuation metrics.

DELIVERY AND LEARNING

The output connects sports event data with a visual reading of individual performance, leaving an analytical notebook on GitHub and a LinkedIn publication.
