Project 02 · Data analytics

Airbnb Analysis in London

✅ published data: 2025

Exploratory analysis of Airbnb lodging in London, focused on pricing, accommodation categories, reviews and territorial patterns.

CONTEXT AND OBJECTIVE

Explore price variations among London Airbnb listings based on accommodation type, neighbourhood, availability and minimum stay.

DATA AND DIAGNOSIS

  • Public Inside Airbnb data is used as the main source.
  • Listings are cleaned, validated and segmented to build a consistent analytical base.
  • Visuals are built to compare prices, categories, neighbourhoods, territorial concentration and recent activity.

DELIVERY AND LEARNING

The project is presented as a reproducible analysis on GitHub and as an editorial piece on LinkedIn, with a visual reading focused on prices, location and the structure of urban lodging.
